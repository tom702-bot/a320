# Ansett A320 Checkride Trainer

An installable study app for the Ansett A320 IAE V2500-A5. Live site: https://tom702-bot.github.io/a320/

The 5 September 2026 FCOM audit reviewed all 259 limitation questions, 315 systems-guide questions, eight memory cards and 141 fill-in cells. The 6 September follow-up rechecked all 102 outstanding records and restored four corrected or qualified questions. On 9 September, all 298 rows from Question Bank.xlsx were added with their supplied answer keys. On 14 September, Systems Exam Prep was restricted to Communications and expanded with 51 questions checked against FCOM DSC-23 and the 12-page Doc1 supplement. The active pool now contains 70 questions: 19 workbook rows and 51 FCOM-checked additions. A presentation-only quality layer replaces giveaway distractors across all 70 active questions with plausible, parallel Communications alternatives while preserving each source-backed correct answer and the original source options. QB159 and the supplement's DFDR, QAR and accelerometer material are excluded because they belong to ATA 31 Indicating/Recording. No independent FCOM-correctness claim is made for the retained workbook answers. Grading also includes 200 limitations and 121 fill-in cells. See [the earlier outstanding-item review](OUTSTANDING_REVIEW.md), verification-audit.json and communications-fcom-audit.json. FCOM source matching is not operational certification or confirmation of the latest revision.

Every question carries a stable source-record ID. FCOM-reviewed questions also carry their FCOM PDF pages; workbook questions carry the workbook source hash and row number. The original results, corrections, exclusions, manual identifiers and page revisions are in verification-audit.json; the Communications additions and their Doc1/FCOM evidence map are in communications-fcom-audit.json. SOURCE_REVIEW.md explains the source scope. Systems guide numbers 311–317 are absent from the supplied guide. All 315 guide records and all 298 workbook records remain archived and traceable, but the active systems pool, weak-area review and mastery counts use only the explicit 70-question Communications allowlist. Withheld limitations remain excluded. The limitation lookup also uses only eligible records.

Both Learn and Exam systems runs share on-device Communications draw history. Undrawn questions come first, followed by the least recently drawn selections. A run never duplicates a question; All 70 includes the entire active pool in a new order. Starting a run reserves its questions in the rotation, including when the run is later abandoned. The history survives reloads, feedback-mode changes and run-length changes. If browser storage is unavailable, it remains in memory until the page closes. Existing answer/mastery keys remain unchanged; Reset progress also clears rotation history.

The IAE limits use A/C 20-IMHT, 13 AUG 2018, PDF pp.3737–3739, marked FOR ENGINEERING USE ONLY. The compilation contains different aircraft and revision inserts; its cover and filename do not override individual effectivity. Numeric IAE minimum oil quantity and consumption allowance remain unresolved.

Memory cards distinguish conditional, simultaneous and follow-up actions. They are self-graded training summaries, not full checklists or competency assessments. Fill & Check accepts equivalent numeric formatting and matching optional units while rejecting wrong units, arbitrary text, missing signs and different ranges.

Thirteen flow stages retain CM1/PM and CM2/PF as the selected training allocation. The virtual cockpit uses the original FlyByWire A320 artwork: complete instrument panel, forward and aft overhead, and full pedestal. Four hundred controls are positioned over the photographed switches and keys, with transparent click targets and hover state labels. Crew/data observations appear in a separate tray. Guided practice follows the next physical control; Explore pauses grading; the control finder locates named controls. Preparation starts powered after preliminary preparation and uses expanded SOP checks and explicit prerequisites. Engine start, after takeoff and securing are included. The catalog contains 480 controls/checks (seven obsolete broad scans are hidden). The picture is a visual reference; Ansett FCOM procedures and grading remain the authority for this trainer.

Electrical, hydraulic and eight IAE engine diagrams remain ungraded illustrative models. Electrical study assumes a settled in-flight configuration above 100 kt, charged batteries and completed emergency-generator coupling; AC ESS alternate feed requires selection because optional automatic switching is not assumed. Hydraulic study excludes unresolved NWS routing, pressure dynamics and accumulator endurance. Engine geometry and arbitrary component-failure propagation are explanatory, not maintenance schematics. Cross-system examples state the assumed source availability.

Learn mode gives immediate feedback; exams defer it to debrief. A pass requires a completed run and an unrounded score of at least 80%. Two consecutive correct recalls are required after a miss. The audited revision invalidates previous question mastery and flow scores. Progress stays on the device.
