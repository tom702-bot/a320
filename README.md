# Ansett A320 Checkride Trainer

An installable, iPhone-friendly study app with a 253-question A320/IAE limitations quiz, memory items, fill-in practice, 270 self-study course questions across nine banks, interactive PF/PM cockpit-flow practice, ECAM-style electrical and hydraulic trainers, and a provisional IAE V2500 engine-systems explorer.

The PF / PM Flow Trainer transcribes the supplied three-page A320 flow-card scan into ten phase drills. Its default Flow Map groups the selected role's unnumbered actions by overhead, main panel, pedestal and visual-check area; correct selections build a numbered coloured path so the physical scan pattern can be learned without showing the answer first. Crew allocation is fixed throughout: CM1 is PM and CM2 is PF. An optional Full Panel view retains more than 300 individually mapped controls, including both MCDUs, EFIS/FCU, ECAM, overhead systems, exterior lights, thrust levers, radar/PWS and ATC/TCAS. Cockpit Preparation starts cold and dark. Inputs are graded correct, incorrect or out of order. Flight-deck artwork/reference: [FlyByWire Simulations A32NX Flight Deck Overview](https://docs.flybywiresim.com/pilots-corner/a32nx/a32nx-briefing/flight-deck/#description); this project is not affiliated with FlyByWire Simulations.

The dedicated Self-Study Quizzes section contains three course areas drawn from the supplied Gas Turbine Engines A320 Self Study Guides (Parts 1 and 2) and Pressurisation A320 Self Study Guide. Each course has three independent 30-question banks, giving a 90-question pool and 270 questions overall. Every attempt serves 30 questions, prioritises questions not yet seen on that device, and only recycles the oldest questions after the course pool is exhausted. These course quizzes are separate from the FCOM limitations audit. Every question uses four fixed A-D choices with same-topic near-miss distractors designed to test precise recall rather than reveal the answer by wording. The app marks each answer, gives an explanation and slide reference, calculates the final score, and stores completed best scores locally on the learner's device.

The Limitations Check contains only material from the supplied FCOM limitations chapter. Airframe and system questions use the A320 pages for A/C 21-CMHT dated 22 MAY 24. Engine questions use only the IAE V2500 appendix for A/C 20-IMHT dated 13 AUG 18; those pages are marked FOR ENGINEERING USE ONLY, and the warning remains visible in the trainer. The separate CFM & PW pages and all non-LIM BTC/systems questions are excluded from Limitations Check.

Existing standalone system trainers, self-study quizzes and memory-item material remain separate and were not revalidated by this limitations-only audit.

The dimensional electrical page lets the learner fail or restore every displayed source, battery, converter, contactor and bus. It recalculates downstream power and explains each transfer, loss or load shed. Five presets remain available for normal, single-generator, APU, emergency-generator and battery-only study. Its architecture and procedural constraints come from the trainer's existing DSC-24 / QRH-referenced content and require confirmation against the current approved Ansett DSC and QRH.

The hydraulic page models the independent green, blue and yellow systems from the supplied 21-CMHT FCOM DSC-29. Every displayed reservoir, pump, fire valve, accumulator, priority valve, leak-measurement valve, pressure circuit and consumer group can be failed or restored. The trainer recalculates PTU power transfer, RAT blue pressure, pressure-dependent user availability and priority-valve shedding, and explains the consequences. Hydraulic fluid is never shown transferring between systems.

The separate IAE V2500 engine page is an interactive systems study assistant. It opens with an original, lightweight 3D cutaway that can be rotated and zoomed on desktop or iPhone. Twenty-three physical selections now cover the inlet, spools, compressor stability devices, diffuser, injectors, igniters, combustor, turbine guide vanes and cooling, bearings, accessory drives, starter, sensors, reverser and exhaust. A switchable overlay traces bypass, core and hot-gas flow, and every part links into its related system view. Eight flow diagrams cover architecture, FADEC/thrust control, fuel, oil, air/start, engine indications/ECAM, compressor stability/stall and the hydraulically powered thrust reverser. Every flow-diagram component can be failed or restored; downstream nodes and paths are recalculated as unavailable or degraded, with an explanation in the component panel. Engine start is retained as one part of the air/start system rather than the main activity. The module is explicitly provisional: engine-specific logic and limits are paraphrased from the supplied IAE engineering appendix for A/C 20-IMHT / 19-IMHE, dated 2015-2018 and marked FOR ENGINEERING USE ONLY; the additional physical, instrumentation and compressor-stability detail is grounded in the supplied 2022 Gas Turbine Engines A320 Self Study Guides. It must be checked against a current approved IAE FCOM before operational use.

This is a study aid only. The current approved FCOM, QRH and company procedures remain authoritative.
