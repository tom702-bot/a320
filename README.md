# Ansett A320 Checkride Trainer

An installable, iPhone-friendly study app with a 315-question A320/IAE Systems Exam Prep bank, a dedicated 259-question limitations quiz, memory items, fill-in practice, interactive PF/PM cockpit-flow practice, ECAM-style electrical and hydraulic trainers, and an IAE V2500 engine-systems explorer.

The PF / PM Flow Trainer transcribes the supplied three-page A320 flow-card scan into ten phase drills. It supports one phase or a continuous sequence with cockpit state carried forward. Its default Flow Map teaches the physical scan, Focused Panel isolates relevant controls, and Full Panel retains the complete 344-control search task. Crew allocation is fixed throughout: CM1 is PM and CM2 is PF. Cockpit Preparation starts cold and dark; Before Start supports external- or APU-power entry configurations. Definitive actions are order-scored, while SET and AS RQRD items are explicitly tracked as conditional acknowledgements. Assisted and unassisted mastery are reported separately. The FlyByWire flight-deck images are used only as an interaction canvas; all procedure and engine content remains scoped to the Ansett A320 IAE V2500. This project is not affiliated with FlyByWire Simulations.

Systems Exam Prep retains all 315 guide question numbers (1–310 and 318–322; 311–317 are absent from the source). The 19 selectable subjects include systems, limitations, MEL and miscellaneous material. Each item displays its source status: seven IAE corrections have explicit checked training references, two MEL items require the current operator MEL, and the remaining 306 retain their references with individual applicability review pending. There is no blanket current-FCOM certification. Learn mode uses adaptive ordering and immediate explanations. Exam mode withholds feedback until debrief, awards a pass only on a completed run, and applies the 80% threshold before rounding. A miss requires two consecutive correct recalls to regain mastery. Corrected items require fresh recall rather than inheriting mastery of the previous answer.

The Limitations Check contains material from the supplied FCOM limitations chapter. Its 35 engine items were checked against the explicit IAE V2500 inserts: A/C 20-IMHT, 13 AUG 2018, LIM-ENG pages 1/4–3/4 (uploaded PDF pages 3737–3739). These pages are marked **FOR ENGINEERING USE ONLY**; the trainer preserves that restriction. The compilation's 21-CMHT cover does not override insert applicability. The remaining 224 airframe/system items retain their references with applicability review pending, including installed-option checks. The requested scope remains Ansett A320 IAE V2500-A5 only. A numeric IAE minimum oil quantity and consumption allowance remain unverified; consult the applicable before-walkaround procedure rather than using another engine's values.

Standalone system trainers and memory-item material remain separate from Systems Exam Prep and the limitations-only check.

The Cross-System Scenario Lab links engine 1 loss, engine 2 loss and dual-engine loss across the IAE power-plant, electrical and hydraulic trainers. Each scenario opens those modules with the relevant preset already selected, making generator/bus-tie, engine-pump/PTU, RAT/blue-hydraulic and emergency-generation dependencies explicit without presenting the lab as an operational checklist.

The dimensional electrical page lets the learner fail or restore every displayed source, battery, converter, contactor and bus. It recalculates downstream power and explains each transfer, loss or load shed. Five presets remain available for normal, single-generator, APU, emergency-generator and battery-only study. Its architecture and procedural constraints come from the trainer's existing DSC-24 / QRH-referenced content and require confirmation against the current approved Ansett DSC and QRH.

The hydraulic page models the independent green, blue and yellow systems from the supplied 21-CMHT FCOM DSC-29. Every displayed reservoir, pump, fire valve, accumulator, priority valve, leak-measurement valve, pressure circuit and consumer group can be failed or restored. The trainer recalculates PTU power transfer, RAT blue pressure, pressure-dependent user availability and priority-valve shedding, and explains the consequences. Hydraulic fluid is never shown transferring between systems.

The separate IAE V2500 engine page has a rotatable, selectable 3D cutaway with 23 parts and eight system diagrams. Engine references use the explicit 20-IMHT IAE training inserts and retain their engineering-use restriction. The supplied 2022 Gas Turbine Engines A320 Self Study Guides supplement the simplified physical, instrumentation and compressor-stability depictions. The current approved FCOM, QRH and ECAM remain authoritative.

This is a study aid only. The current approved FCOM, QRH, MEL and company procedures remain authoritative.

Run `node validate-trainer.js` and `node test-trainer.js` before publishing. The checks cover question structure, reviewed evidence records, source restrictions, scope, flows, controls, scoring, stable answer semantics, progress invalidation and fresh offline asset loading. They do not certify operational applicability. The same commands run in GitHub Actions. The v37 offline manifest and flow-script URLs match; failed script requests never receive an HTML fallback.

The separate PXS and GTE self-study courses were removed at the user's request, together with their menu, scoring screens, question-bank file and offline cache entry.
