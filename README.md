# Ansett A320 Checkride Trainer

An installable, iPhone-friendly study app with a 315-question A320/IAE Systems Exam Prep bank, a dedicated 253-question limitations quiz, memory items, fill-in practice, 270 self-study course questions across nine banks, interactive PF/PM cockpit-flow practice, ECAM-style electrical and hydraulic trainers, and an IAE V2500 engine-systems explorer.

The PF / PM Flow Trainer transcribes the supplied three-page A320 flow-card scan into ten phase drills. It supports one phase or a continuous sequence with cockpit state carried forward. Its default Flow Map teaches the physical scan, Focused Panel isolates relevant controls, and Full Panel retains the complete 344-control search task. Crew allocation is fixed throughout: CM1 is PM and CM2 is PF. Cockpit Preparation starts cold and dark; Before Start supports external- or APU-power entry configurations. Definitive actions are order-scored, while SET and AS RQRD items are explicitly tracked as conditional acknowledgements. Assisted and unassisted mastery are reported separately. The FlyByWire flight-deck images are used only as an interaction canvas; all procedure and engine content remains scoped to the Ansett A320 IAE V2500. This project is not affiliated with FlyByWire Simulations.

The dedicated Self-Study Quizzes section contains three course areas drawn from the supplied Gas Turbine Engines A320 Self Study Guides (Parts 1 and 2) and Pressurisation A320 Self Study Guide. Each course has three independent 30-question banks, giving a 90-question pool and 270 questions overall. Every attempt serves 30 questions, prioritises questions not yet seen on that device, and only recycles the oldest questions after the course pool is exhausted. These course quizzes are separate from the FCOM limitations audit. Every question uses four fixed A-D choices with same-topic near-miss distractors designed to test precise recall rather than reveal the answer by wording. The app marks each answer, gives an explanation and slide reference, calculates the final score, and stores completed best scores locally on the learner's device.

Systems Exam Prep uses all 315 questions present in the supplied *A320 Student Study Questions* PDF (guide numbers 1–310 and 318–322; 311–317 are absent from the source). The 19 selectable subjects include the guide's systems, limitations, MEL and miscellaneous material. Legacy answers were reconciled to the current AAT A320 FCOM for A/C 21-CMHT, custom issue 16 MAR 2026; items that require operator-specific MEL authority are clearly marked. Learn mode uses an adaptive weak/unseen/not-yet-mastered order and immediate explanations. Exam mode withholds correctness, scores and references until the final debrief and applies an 80% pass threshold. A miss requires two consecutive correct recalls to regain mastery.

The Limitations Check contains only material from the supplied FCOM limitations chapter. Airframe, system and IAE V2500 engine values use the AAT A320 FCOM for A/C 21-CMHT, custom issue 16 MAR 2026 (Airbus content issue 22 MAY 2024). No aircraft or engine variant outside the Ansett A320 IAE V2500-A5 scope is included.

Standalone system trainers, course self-study quizzes and memory-item material remain separate from Systems Exam Prep and the limitations-only check.

The Cross-System Scenario Lab links engine 1 loss, engine 2 loss and dual-engine loss across the IAE power-plant, electrical and hydraulic trainers. Each scenario opens those modules with the relevant preset already selected, making generator/bus-tie, engine-pump/PTU, RAT/blue-hydraulic and emergency-generation dependencies explicit without presenting the lab as an operational checklist.

The dimensional electrical page lets the learner fail or restore every displayed source, battery, converter, contactor and bus. It recalculates downstream power and explains each transfer, loss or load shed. Five presets remain available for normal, single-generator, APU, emergency-generator and battery-only study. Its architecture and procedural constraints come from the trainer's existing DSC-24 / QRH-referenced content and require confirmation against the current approved Ansett DSC and QRH.

The hydraulic page models the independent green, blue and yellow systems from the supplied 21-CMHT FCOM DSC-29. Every displayed reservoir, pump, fire valve, accumulator, priority valve, leak-measurement valve, pressure circuit and consumer group can be failed or restored. The trainer recalculates PTU power transfer, RAT blue pressure, pressure-dependent user availability and priority-valve shedding, and explains the consequences. Hydraulic fluid is never shown transferring between systems.

The separate IAE V2500 engine page is an interactive systems study assistant. It opens with an original, lightweight 3D cutaway that can be rotated and zoomed on desktop or iPhone. Twenty-three physical selections cover the inlet, spools, compressor stability devices, diffuser, injectors, igniters, combustor, turbine guide vanes and cooling, bearings, accessory drives, starter, sensors, reverser and exhaust. Eight flow diagrams cover architecture, FADEC/thrust control, fuel, oil, air/start, engine indications/ECAM, compressor stability/stall and the hydraulically powered thrust reverser. Current limits come from the AAT A320 FCOM for A/C 21-CMHT; the supplied 2022 Gas Turbine Engines A320 Self Study Guides supplement the simplified physical, instrumentation and compressor-stability depictions. The current approved FCOM, QRH and ECAM remain authoritative.

This is a study aid only. The current approved FCOM, QRH, MEL and company procedures remain authoritative.

Run `node validate-trainer.js` before publishing. The validator checks question counts and references, duplicate questions and choices, strict aircraft/engine scope, flow-state behavior, cockpit-control geometry, engine explorer links, the integrated scenario presets, offline-cache versioning and parity between the two main HTML entry files. The same validation runs automatically in GitHub Actions.
