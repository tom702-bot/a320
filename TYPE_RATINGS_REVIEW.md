# Pilot Type Ratings review — 1 October 2026

Reviewed all 16 existing questions and the study notes against the two supplied
whiteboard photos (IMG_6090.jpeg and IMG_6091.jpeg), the Register's compilation
102, and the relevant 2026 amending instrument. The resulting bank has 20 questions.

## Findings and changes

- The 61.770 privilege, 61.775 simulator-test applicability, 25-hour **as pilot**
  requirement, PIC restriction, and the 1,000-hour / 2,000-plus-500-hour turbojet
  alternatives were correct.
- The photo's early-renewal **OR** is a transcription error: 61.800(2)(b) requires
  both a still-valid review and expiry within three months. The trainer already
  used AND. TR18–TR19 now cover expired and more-than-three-months-early reviews.
- Schedule 1 item 15 changed **class** to **type** in both 61.800(4)(b) and (c)
  from 30 June 2026. The existing question/notes correctly applied this to the
  operational-rating flight test, but the correction notice did not mention
  design-feature training. The notice now names both; TR17 tests the latter.
- The notes and TR15 omitted 202.291(1): the new 61.800(5A) credits between
  ratings apply to flight reviews successfully completed on or after 30 June
  2026. TR15 now has an explicit post-commencement scenario, the notes include
  the condition, and TR20 tests it. These two answers link to the amending
  instrument containing the application rule.
- TR05 had an ambiguous distractor: 1,000 PIC hours on the particular turbojet
  type would also meet the stated experience threshold. Replaced it and kept
  one defensible answer. TR08 now expressly identifies a turbojet type rating
  and simulator flight test.
