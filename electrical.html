NORMAL CONFIGURATION

GEN 1 supplies AC BUS 1 and GEN 2 supplies AC BUS 2. AC BUS 1 normally supplies AC ESS. Transformer rectifiers convert AC power to 28 V DC for the DC network.

HOW EACH SECTION WORKS

IDG / ENGINE GENERATOR

The constant-speed drive turns changing engine speed into constant-frequency AC. The GCU regulates and protects generator output.

AC BUS 1 / AC BUS 2

The main 115/200 V, 400 Hz distribution buses. Bus-tie contactors let one available source supply both buses when required.

AC ESS BUS

Feeds priority AC equipment. It normally takes power from AC BUS 1 and has an alternate supply path.

TR 1 / TR 2 / ESS TR

Transformer rectifiers convert AC into 28 V DC. The ESS TR provides an alternate or emergency path to the essential DC network.

DC BUS 1 / DC BUS 2

Distribute normal DC power. DC ESS preserves priority DC equipment when the main network is degraded.

HOT BUSES / DC BAT BUS

Each hot bus remains directly connected to its battery. The DC BAT BUS links battery charging and essential battery-fed distribution through the battery and DC-tie contactors.

AC/DC ESS SHED BUSES

Feed lower-priority essential loads in normal operation. They are automatically shed when the aircraft is reduced to emergency or battery-only power.

CONTACTORS

Generator-line, bus-tie, battery, DC-tie and AC ESS-feed contactors automatically connect or isolate sources and buses during reconfiguration.

RAT / EMER GEN

After loss of both main AC buses in flight, the RAT provides hydraulic power to the emergency generator, which supplies the essential network.

BATTERIES / STATIC INVERTER

The hot buses remain directly battery-connected. In battery-only operation, the static inverter converts battery DC into essential AC power.

RATINGS & OPERATING CONSTRAINTS

MAIN GENERATORS

Each engine-driven generator is rated at 90 kVA and supplies 115/200 V, 400 Hz AC. The APU generator can replace either or both main generators.

TRANSFORMER RECTIFIERS

TR 1, TR 2 and the ESS TR convert AC to 28 V DC; each main TR is rated to 200 A.

IDG DISCONNECT — MAX 3 SECONDS

Do not hold the IDG disconnect pushbutton for more than 3 seconds. An IDG disconnected in flight cannot be reconnected by the crew.

CIRCUIT BREAKER RESET

A circuit breaker may be reset only once and only when an approved procedure authorises the reset.

LOSS OF BOTH MAIN AC BUSES

Above 100 kt, the RAT automatically deploys and the aircraft changes to the emergency electrical configuration. Essential loads only remain supplied.

SINGLE GENERATOR FAILURE

Follow ECAM. Starting the APU restores generator redundancy if the failed engine generator cannot be recovered.
