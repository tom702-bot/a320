    IAE V2500 ENGINE LIMITS

    EGT / THRUST TIME

    Takeoff, go-around and starting: 635 °C. MCT: 610 °C. Takeoff/go-around: 5 minutes normally or 10 minutes with one engine inoperative.

    SHAFT SPEED

    Maximum N1: 100%. Maximum N2: 100%.

    OIL

    Maximum continuous temperature: 155 °C. Maximum transient: 165 °C for 15 minutes. Minimum: −40 °C for start, −10 °C before exceeding idle and 50 °C before takeoff. Minimum pressure: 60 PSI.

    REVERSE

    Reverse is prohibited in flight and must not be used to back the aircraft. Maximum reverse must not be used below 70 kt; idle reverse is permitted to a stop.

    CONTROL & PROTECTION NOTES

    EPR / N1 REVERSION

    EPR is the normal thrust-control mode. Loss of sensed EPR produces rated N1 mode; loss of computed EPR produces degraded N1 mode. Autothrust and alpha-floor protection are lost in either N1 reversion.

    AUTOMATIC START

    Below 50% N2 on the ground, FADEC can abort a hot, hung, stalled or no-light-up automatic start and command approximately 30 seconds of dry cranking. A manual start is monitored but not automatically aborted.

    STARTER

    Allow 15 seconds between cycles and 30 minutes after three failed cycles or five minutes of continuous cranking. Maximum manual-start cycle: 2 minutes; crank: 5 minutes. Starter use is prohibited above 10% N2 on the ground or 18% in flight.

    GROUND START

    The supplied IAE engineering limitation states that the engine can be started in crosswind up to 35 kt.
