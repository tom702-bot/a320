AUTOMATIC START SEQUENCE

  1. ENG MODE to IGN/START; ENG SD page appears and packs close.
  2. ENG MASTER ON; LP fuel valve and start valve open.
  3. Starter accelerates N2; dry crank completes if required.
  4. At 10-16% N2, HP fuel opens and one igniter operates.
  5. Light-up: EGT and N1 rise as the engine accelerates.
  6. Above 43% N2, start valve closes and ignition stops.
  7. Engine stabilises at idle; AVAIL is displayed.

READY FOR ENGINE START

Select IGN/START, then move ENG 2 MASTER to ON. Use NEXT STEP to study each action or AUTO RUN for a continuous demonstration.

  • The fault selector changes what FADEC encounters during the start.
  • Component isolations override the selected scenario.

COMPONENT AVAILABILITY

AVAILABLE means the component can respond to FADEC or cockpit commands. ISOLATED removes it from the model. Selecting both FADEC channels off prevents automatic start control.

IAE V2500 ENGINE LIMITS

EGT

Takeoff/go-around and starting: 635 °C. Maximum continuous thrust: 610 °C. Takeoff/go-around time: 5 minutes normally, 10 minutes with one engine inoperative.

SHAFT SPEED

Maximum N1: 100%. Maximum N2: 100%.

OIL

Maximum continuous temperature: 155 °C. Maximum transient: 165 °C for 15 minutes. Minimum starting temperature: -40 °C; minimum before idle is exceeded: -10 °C; minimum before takeoff: 50 °C. Minimum oil pressure: 60 PSI.

REVERSE

Reverse selection is prohibited in flight and must not be used to back the aircraft. Maximum reverse should not be used below 70 kt; idle reverse is permitted to a stop.

STARTER & FADEC LOGIC

STARTER CYCLES

One automatic attempt is one cycle. Allow 15 seconds between ground-start cycles and 30 minutes after three failed cycles or five minutes of continuous cranking.

MANUAL / CRANK

Maximum manual-start cycle: 2 minutes. Maximum crank cycle: 5 minutes. Do not run the starter above 10% N2 on the ground or 18% in flight.

AUTOMATIC PROTECTION

During an automatic ground start below 50% N2, FADEC detects hot, hung, stall or no-light-up conditions, closes the HP fuel and start valves, stops ignition and commands a dry crank of approximately 30 seconds.

MANUAL START

FADEC monitors a manual start but does not automatically abort it. The flight crew must respond to the ECAM warning and interrupt the sequence.

GROUND CROSSWIND

The supplied IAE engineering limitation states that the engine can be started in crosswind up to 35 kt.
