    SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

    THREE INDEPENDENT SYSTEMS

    Green, blue and yellow each have their own reservoir. Hydraulic fluid cannot transfer between systems.

    GREEN / YELLOW

    Engine 1 drives the green pump and engine 2 drives the yellow pump. The bidirectional PTU transfers mechanical power between these systems, not fluid.

    BLUE / RAT

    The blue electric pump normally pressurises the blue system. The RAT-driven pump supplies blue hydraulics after loss of both AC buses or manual deployment.

    YELLOW AUXILIARIES

    The yellow electric pump permits ground operation with engines stopped. A hand pump operates the cargo doors without electrical power.

    LIMITS & AUTOMATIC LOGIC

    NORMAL PRESSURE — 3,000 PSI

    RAT-powered blue pressure is 2,500 PSI. ECAM pressure indications become amber below 1,450 PSI.

    PTU — 500 PSI DIFFERENTIAL

    The PTU runs automatically when green/yellow differential pressure exceeds 500 PSI. It is inhibited during the first engine start and tested during the second.

    PRIORITY VALVES

    When system pressure is low, priority valves remove heavy-load users to preserve the primary hydraulic services.

    RESERVOIR PRESSURISATION

    Engine 1 HP bleed normally pressurises all three reservoirs; the crossbleed duct supplies them if that pressure is too low.
